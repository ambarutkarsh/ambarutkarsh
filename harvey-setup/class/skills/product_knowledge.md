# Product Knowledge: Class: AI Product Management

## What We Sell
Class is a 6-month AI Product Management course taught by Ambar Utkarsh, for students and professionals who want to move into AI PM roles. Class Pro gives lifetime access to every course artifact for 5k.

## Core Value Proposition
Most people trying to break into AI product management piece it together from scattered videos. Class gives a structured 6-month path and the real working artifacts an AI PM uses, taught by a practitioner.

## Key Benefits
- A structured 6-month path from current role to AI Product Manager
- Reusable artifacts: AI PRDs, evaluation plans, model-selection and launch templates
- Portfolio-ready projects for AI PM interviews
- Learn from a practitioner, not a generic MOOC
