# Product Knowledge: RetentionOS

## What We Sell
RetentionOS is a retention operating system for health insurance companies. It predicts which policies are likely to lapse or not renew, explains why, and drives the outreach (agent calls, broker nudges, member messages) that saves them.

## Core Value Proposition
Health insurers lose a large share of premium at renewal, and most only find out after the policy has lapsed. RetentionOS moves retention from reactive lapse reporting to a prioritised save list 60-90 days ahead of renewal.

## Key Benefits
- Flags at-risk policies 60-90 days before renewal
- Raises renewal and persistency rates without adding retention headcount
- One prioritised save list for agents, brokers and call centres, with the reason each member is at risk
- Shows which save actions work, by segment and channel

## Features
- Renewal and lapse risk scoring per policy
- Risk drivers per member (premium increase, claim experience, service complaints, payment failures, low engagement)
- Save playbooks by segment and channel
- Agent/broker work queues and member outreach triggers
- Renewal-rate and saved-premium reporting

## Use Cases
- Retail individual and family health plans with weak renewal rates
- Group/corporate health renewals at risk of moving to a competitor
- Persistency improvement programmes (common in India and Southeast Asia)
- Post-premium-revision churn after a price increase

## Pricing
Annual platform license sized to policies under management. Pilot on one product line available. A free renewal-leakage diagnostic is the entry offer.

## Target Audience
Health insurers, health plans, HMOs and insurance TPAs worldwide, especially those whose renewal or persistency rates are falling.

## Ideal Buyer Persona
The executive who owns renewal numbers: Chief Customer Officer, Head of Retention / Renewals / Persistency, Chief Distribution Officer, Head of Customer Experience, or COO. They report renewal rate to the board and are short on early warning.

## Integrations
- Policy administration and core insurance systems (via data export or API)
- CRM and contact-centre platforms
- Email, SMS and WhatsApp messaging

## Prospect Pain Points
- Renewal or persistency rate falling, often after premium increases
- Lapses discovered only after the grace period ends
- Agents and brokers don't know which members to call first
- Retention budget spent evenly instead of on the members at risk
- No clear measure of which save actions work

## Buying Triggers
When these events happen, prospects are most likely to buy:
- Hiring a Head of Retention, Renewals Manager or Persistency Manager
- Announced premium revisions or repricing
- Regulatory or board pressure on persistency ratios (e.g. IRDAI persistency disclosures in India)
- New CX or digital transformation leader appointed
- Weak renewal or persistency numbers in public disclosures

## Disqualifiers
Do NOT pursue prospects who:
- Are not insurers, health plans, HMOs or TPAs (e.g. hospitals, clinics, brokers with no book of their own)
- Only sell life, motor or property insurance with no health line
- Are very small (fewer than ~200 employees) or pre-launch

## Market Positioning
The renewal-specific retention layer for health insurers: earlier warning than lapse reports, more actionable than a generic data-science model.

## Tone & Voice
Consultative, data-driven, concise. Use insurance terms (renewal rate, persistency, lapse, grace period, book of business). Never make claims about results we can't back up.
