# Competitive Battle Cards: RetentionOS

Use these when a prospect mentions an alternative or asks "how are you different?"

## Market Positioning
The renewal-specific retention layer for health insurers.

## Our Unique Strengths
- Health insurance renewal focus
- Risk score tied to a specific save action and owner
- Pilot on de-identified data

## Common Alternatives (Non-Competitors)
What prospects do instead of buying a solution like ours:
- Monthly lapse reports from the policy admin system
- In-house data science churn models that never reach the front line
- Blanket renewal reminder campaigns sent to every member
- CRM workflows with no risk prioritisation

---

## vs. In-house churn model

**How we win:** We turn the score into a prioritised save list and playbook the retention team actually works from, and measure which actions save policies.

**Their weakness:** Models often stay in a dashboard; the front line never changes what it does.

**Migration angle:** Keep your model as an input. RetentionOS adds the action and measurement layer.

---

## vs. Generic CRM / customer success platform

**How we win:** Built around policy renewal cycles, grace periods and health-insurance risk drivers rather than SaaS subscription churn.

**Their weakness:** Needs heavy configuration to understand insurance renewals.

**Migration angle:** RetentionOS feeds prioritised tasks into the CRM you already have.
