# Cold email rules

## Shape
- First email: 50-110 words. Follow-ups: 25-70 words.
- Subject: 2-5 words, lower case is fine, no clickbait, no emoji, no "quick question".
- One idea per email, one ask per email.
- Plain text. No bullet lists in the first email, no links except the booking link when the goal
  is a call, no attachments, no images.

## Structure of the first email
1. Open with the hook: something specific and true about them or their organisation. Never open
   with "I hope this finds you well", "My name is", or a compliment.
2. Connect it to a problem they probably have, in their own language.
3. One or two sentences on what we do about it. Concrete, not adjectives.
4. A low-effort ask tied to the ENTRY OFFER (a question they can answer in one line is best).

## Follow-ups
- Follow-up 1: a new angle or a useful detail, not "just bumping this".
- Last follow-up: short and gracious, makes it easy to say "not now" or point to the right person.

## Voice
- Write like a thoughtful founder writing to one person. Short sentences. Contractions are fine.
- No hype words: revolutionary, cutting-edge, game-changing, leverage, synergy, unlock, supercharge,
  seamless, delve, empower, robust.
- No exclamation marks. No em dashes.
- Never invent facts, customers, results, numbers, mutual connections or prior conversations.
- Never pretend to be a person other than the sender, or that the email is not a sales email.
- Never mention pricing in the first email unless the offer is a low fixed price that helps
  (e.g. Class Pro to an individual).
- Never include text marked TODO.
