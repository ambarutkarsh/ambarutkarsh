# RetentionOS: what Closer should know

## The problem we solve
Health insurers lose a large share of premium at renewal, and most only find out after a policy
has lapsed past its grace period. Retention teams work from monthly lapse reports, send the same
reminder to every member, and can't tell which save actions work.

## What RetentionOS does
- Scores every policy for renewal/lapse risk 60-90 days before renewal
- Explains the driver for each member: premium increase, claim experience, service complaints,
  payment failures, low engagement
- Turns scores into a prioritised save list for agents, brokers and the contact centre, plus
  triggered member messages (email, SMS, WhatsApp)
- Measures which save actions work, by segment and channel

## Who feels the pain
The executive who reports renewal or persistency rate to the board: Chief Customer Officer,
Head of Retention/Renewals/Persistency, Chief Distribution Officer, Head of CX, COO.

## Timing signals worth mentioning
- Hiring for a retention, renewals or persistency role
- A recent premium revision or repricing
- Regulator or board attention on persistency (e.g. IRDAI persistency disclosures in India)
- A new CX, distribution or digital leader
- Weak renewal or persistency numbers in public disclosures

## Common objections and how to answer
- "We already have lapse reporting / a CRM": reports say who lapsed; we say who will, early enough to act, and feed the CRM you have.
- "Our data science team built a churn model": keep it as an input; we add the action and measurement layer that gets it used.
- "Health data is sensitive": the pilot runs on de-identified policy data and respects data-residency needs.
- "Too expensive": the diagnostic is free, and a pilot on one product line shows the number before any commitment.
