# Class: what Closer should know

## The problem we solve
Many students and professionals want to move into AI product management but piece it together
from scattered videos. Generic PM courses treat AI as one module, and learners finish with
nothing to show in an AI PM interview.

## What Class is
- A 6-month AI Product Management course taught by Ambar Utkarsh, a practitioner
- Class Pro: lifetime access to every course artifact for 5,000: AI PRDs, evaluation plans,
  model-selection and launch templates, case studies, frameworks
- Portfolio-ready project work

## Who it is for
Engineers, analysts, consultants and PMs, usually 1-8 years into their career, and final-year
students targeting APM roles.

## Why institutions and companies care
- Placement teams: AI PM is one of the roles students ask about most; a session or programme
  gives them a concrete path.
- Product clubs and communities: a free session on breaking into AI PM is useful content for members.
- L&D leaders: moving existing PMs, engineers and analysts into AI product roles is cheaper than hiring.

## What we can offer an institution or community
A free session on moving into AI product management, sample artifacts for their members, and
group access to Class or Class Pro. (Group pricing: discuss on a call; never quote a number.)

## Common objections and how to answer
- "Too expensive": Class Pro is the low-cost way to start; the full course can come later.
- "No time": the course is designed around a full-time job; Class Pro is fully self-paced.
- "It's all free online": what's hard to find is a sequenced path and real AI PM artifacts.
- "Not sure AI PM is for me": share a sample artifact so they can see what the work looks like.

## Proof
No published student outcomes yet. Never invent testimonials, placements, salaries or numbers.
